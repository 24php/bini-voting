👋 Welcome to BINI Voting Team!

BINI Voting Team (BVT) is the official voting team under Team Bloom Philippines, dedicated to organizing and coordinating voting initiatives to support BINI across various award-giving bodies and recognition platforms.

This serves as a centralized hub for all things voting related to BINI. Here, BLOOMs can find compiled voting links for ongoing campaigns, access relevant voting resources, and stay updated on opportunities to support our girls. Our goal is to make voting information accessible and help streamline the community’s collective efforts.

BLOOMs, thank you for being part of our efforts to support BINI. Together, let’s continue making every vote count!

Lagi’t lagi, #ParaSaBINI! ❀
